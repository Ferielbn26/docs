This quickstart guide walks you through everything you need to begin learning on EcoLearn right away. By the end, you will have a fully configured account, a clear picture of your personalized dashboard, and hands-on experience with your first lesson and simulation. No prior setup is required — just a browser and a few minutes of your time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Navigate to [app.ecolearn.app](https://app.ecolearn.app) and click **Sign Up** in the top-right corner. You can register using your email address and a password, or sign in instantly with your existing **Google** or **Microsoft** account via single sign-on (SSO).

    During sign-up, choose your role:
    - **Student** — access lessons, simulations, assignments, and personal progress tracking.
    - **Educator** — unlock class management, assignment creation, and student analytics on top of everything students can access.

    Once you confirm your email address, your account is active and ready to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Profile">
    After logging in for the first time, EcoLearn prompts you to complete your profile. Fill in the following details to personalize your experience:

    - **Display Name** — the name shown on your dashboard, certificates, and to classmates.
    - **Grade Level or Subject Area** — helps EcoLearn surface lessons and simulations most relevant to you. Students select a grade range; educators specify the subject and grade levels they teach.
    - **Learning Goals** — choose from preset goals such as *Prepare for AP Environmental Science*, *Explore Biodiversity*, or *Support Classroom Instruction*, or write your own.

    You can update all of these settings at any time from **Account → Profile Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore Your Dashboard">
    Your dashboard is the central hub for everything on EcoLearn. Here is a quick overview of each section:

    - **My Lessons** — your assigned and in-progress lessons, organized by topic and completion status.
    - **Simulations** — a library of interactive ecological models you can run and replay freely.
    - **Progress** — a visual summary of completed content, quiz scores, time spent, and learning streaks.
    - **Assignments** — tasks assigned by your educator, complete with due dates and submission status. *(Students only — educators see a mirrored view for managing and grading.)*

    Spend a moment clicking through each section to get your bearings before diving in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Your First Lesson">
    Ready to learn? From the dashboard, go to **My Lessons → Terrestrial Ecosystems** and click **Begin**. The lesson opens in a full-screen reader with embedded interactive activities, diagrams, and short knowledge checks along the way.

    Work through each section at your own pace. EcoLearn automatically saves your progress, so you can pause and return exactly where you left off at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try a Simulation">
    Once you have a taste of the lessons, head to **Simulations → Population Dynamics**. This simulation lets you model how predator and prey populations interact over time.

    - Use the sliders to adjust starting population sizes, birth rates, and resource availability.
    - Click **Run Simulation** to watch population curves evolve across generations.
    - Tweak one variable at a time and observe how even small changes cascade through the ecosystem.

    Each simulation includes a brief guided prompt to help you connect what you observe back to the ecological concepts you are studying.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If your teacher has set up a class on EcoLearn, ask them for your **Class Code**. Enter it under **Dashboard → Join a Class** to automatically sync your account with your class roster, receive assigned lessons, and let your teacher track your progress.
</Tip>
