Every lesson, simulation, and quiz in EcoLearn is tagged to one or more academic standards frameworks so you can be confident that the content you assign directly supports the curriculum you're required to teach. Whether your school follows NGSS, prepares students for AP or IB exams, or integrates science literacy into an ELA or math context, EcoLearn's alignment tools help you find the right content quickly and document curriculum coverage for administrators.

## Supported Standards Frameworks

| Framework | Scope | Grade Levels |
|---|---|---|
| **NGSS** (Next Generation Science Standards) | Life sciences, Earth sciences, and engineering practices with a focus on phenomena-based learning | K–12 |
| **AP Environmental Science** | Advanced Placement curriculum covering ecosystems, biodiversity, land use, energy, pollution, and global change | Grades 11–12 |
| **IB Biology** | International Baccalaureate curriculum covering ecology, evolution, and biological systems in depth | Grades 11–12 |
| **Common Core ELA** | Reading and writing in a science context — informational text, evidence-based argument, and scientific communication | K–12 |
| **CCSS Math** | Data analysis and quantitative reasoning applied to ecology contexts — graphing population data, calculating rates, interpreting models | Grades 6–12 |

## Finding Content by Standard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Content Library">
    From your **Educator Dashboard**, click **Content Library** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by standard">
    Click **Filter by Standard** in the filter panel on the left side of the library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your framework">
    Choose your standards framework from the dropdown — for example, **NGSS** or **AP Environmental Science**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse or search for a specific standard">
    Scroll through the listed standards or type a specific **standards code** into the search box (for example, `HS-LS2-1` for NGSS, or `ERT-1.A` for AP Environmental Science). The library immediately filters to show only content aligned to that code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore aligned content">
    Click any standard in the list to expand it and see all **lessons**, **simulations**, and **quizzes** aligned to that standard. Click any item to preview it or add it directly to an assignment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Standards Tags on Content

Every piece of content in EcoLearn displays its standards tags on the content card and at the top of the preview panel. Tags appear in a compact format — for example, `NGSS: HS-LS2-1`, `APES: ERT-2.B`, or `IB Bio: C.3`.

Click any tag to instantly filter the Content Library to everything aligned to that same standard. This is a fast way to discover related lessons or simulations you may not have encountered yet, and to build multi-day unit sequences around a single standard thread.

<Tip>
  Use the **Curriculum Planner** (Educator Dashboard → Curriculum Planner) to map an entire semester at once. Select your framework and grade level, drag standards into a weekly calendar, and EcoLearn suggests content for each slot. You can then assign everything directly from the planner without leaving the page.
</Tip>
