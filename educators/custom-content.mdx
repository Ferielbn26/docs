EcoLearn's pre-built content library covers a wide range of ecology topics, but every classroom has unique needs — a local watershed case study, a unit tied to a recent environmental news event, or a quiz written to match your exact assessment language. The Custom Content editor lets you build lessons, quizzes, and simulation scenarios from scratch and assign them to your class exactly like any pre-built item. Everything you create lives in **My Content** and is visible only to you unless you choose to share it.

## Creating a Custom Lesson

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the lesson editor">
    From your **Educator Dashboard**, go to **My Content → New Lesson**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a title and learning objective">
    Give your lesson a clear **title** and write at least one **learning objective** in student-facing language (for example, "Students will be able to explain how energy flows through a food web"). Objectives appear at the top of the lesson for students.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the lesson content">
    Use the block-based **content editor** to add:
    - **Text blocks** — formatted with headings, bullet points, bold/italic, and inline links.
    - **Images** — upload from your device or paste a URL.
    - **Videos** — upload an MP4 file or paste a YouTube embed URL.
    - **Interactive activities** — choose from drag-and-drop sorting, image hotspot labeling, or sequencing exercises using EcoLearn's built-in activity templates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an end-of-lesson quiz (optional)">
    Click **Add Quiz** at the bottom of the lesson editor to append a short comprehension check. Use the question builder to write questions in any supported format.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish to your library">
    Click **Publish to My Library**. The lesson is now available in **My Content** and can be selected when creating a new assignment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Creating a Custom Quiz

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the quiz editor">
    From **My Content**, click **New Quiz**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter quiz details">
    Provide a **quiz title** and an optional **description** that students see before they begin (for example, instructions or a reminder about calculator use).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add questions">
    Click **Add Question** and use the **question builder** to:
    - Choose a **question type** (multiple choice, true/false, matching, fill-in-the-blank, or short answer).
    - Enter the **question text** and any supporting image or diagram.
    - Enter **answer choices** and **mark the correct answer** (for objective types).
    - Add an optional **hint** students can reveal during the quiz, and an **explanation** shown after submission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a time limit (optional)">
    Toggle on **Time Limit** and enter a duration in minutes if you want the quiz to automatically submit when time expires.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the quiz">
    Click **Save**. The quiz appears in **My Content** and is ready to assign. You can edit it at any time before it's been submitted by students.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sharing Custom Content

By default, everything you create is private to your account. You have three sharing options:

- **Assign to your class** — select the content when creating an assignment and publish it as normal. Only students in that class can access it.
- **Share with your school** — click **Share → School Library** on any custom item to make it discoverable by other verified educators at your school. They can preview and use your content but cannot edit it.
- **Keep private** — leave sharing off entirely. The content remains in **My Content** with a **Private** badge and is never visible to other educators or students unless you assign it.

<Warning>
  Do not include student personal data — such as names, grades, photos, or any other personally identifiable information — in custom content you share to the School Library. Shared content may be viewed by other educators and, depending on your school's settings, across multiple class sections.
</Warning>
