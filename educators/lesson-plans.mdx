EcoLearn's Lesson Plan Library gives you fully developed, classroom-ready plans that you can drop into your schedule with minimal setup. Each plan is built around EcoLearn's interactive content — lessons, simulations, and quizzes — so every activity you see in a plan is one click away from being assigned to your class. Whether you need a single 45-minute class session or a multi-day unit, the library has options at every scale and grade level.

## Browsing Lesson Plans

The **Lesson Plan Library** is accessible from **Educator Dashboard → Lesson Plans**. Use the search bar and filters to narrow your options:

- **Topic** — for example, food webs, biodiversity, climate and ecosystems, or water cycles.
- **Grade level** — filter by K–5, 6–8, 9–10, or 11–12 to match your students' age and prior knowledge.
- **Duration** — find single-period plans (45–50 min), double-period plans (90 min), or multi-day units (3–5 days).
- **Standard** — filter by NGSS code, AP Environmental Science unit, or IB Biology topic to ensure alignment with your curriculum.

Each plan card in the library shows the **title**, **estimated duration**, **grade level**, **key standards tags**, and a brief description. Click any card to open the full preview before committing to use it.

Every lesson plan in the library includes:

- **Learning objectives** — clear, measurable outcomes written in student-facing language.
- **Materials list** — any physical materials needed alongside EcoLearn (handouts, lab supplies, etc.) plus all digital EcoLearn content linked directly.
- **Estimated time** — broken down by activity so you can adjust pacing for your class.
- **EcoLearn content to assign** — direct links to the specific lessons, simulations, or quizzes built into the plan.
- **Discussion questions** — open-ended prompts to spark class conversation before, during, and after activities.

## Using a Lesson Plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Lesson Plan Library">
    From your **Educator Dashboard**, click **Lesson Plans** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse or search">
    Use the filters or search bar to find plans relevant to your current unit. Narrow by topic, grade level, duration, or standards code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview the plan">
    Click **Preview** on any plan card to review the full plan in detail — objectives, activity sequence, time estimates, discussion questions, and all linked EcoLearn content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to your calendar">
    Click **Use This Plan** to copy the plan into your **Teaching Calendar**. Select the class it's for and your preferred start date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize as needed">
    Once added to your calendar, click **Edit Plan** to adjust the learning objectives, swap in different EcoLearn content, remove activities that don't fit your context, or add your own discussion questions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule the plan">
    Confirm the **date** and **class section**, then click **Schedule**. Any EcoLearn assignments embedded in the plan are automatically queued in your Assignments tab with the dates you've set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Lesson Plan Components

Most plans in the library follow a consistent five-part structure that fits within a standard class period:

| Component | Typical Duration | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm-Up Activity** | 5 min | Activate prior knowledge with a quick question, image prompt, or short poll. |
| **Introduce Concept** | 10 min | Direct instruction or a brief video to frame the topic before students explore. |
| **EcoLearn Lesson / Simulation** | 20–30 min | The core interactive work students complete independently or in pairs. |
| **Class Discussion** | 10 min | Debrief findings, address misconceptions, and connect content to real-world examples. |
| **Exit Quiz** | 5 min | A short 3–5 question formative check to gauge understanding before the next session. |

<Note>
  You can duplicate any lesson plan and customize the copy without affecting the original. Go to the plan in your Teaching Calendar, click the **⋯** menu, and select **Duplicate**. Your personalized version is saved to **My Lesson Plans** and never overwritten by EcoLearn updates.
</Note>
