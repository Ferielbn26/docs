Assignments are how you push EcoLearn content into your students' queues with clear expectations, deadlines, and point values attached. You can assign anything in the Content Library — pre-built lessons, interactive simulations, auto-graded quizzes, or your own custom material — and target the whole class or individual students who need differentiated work.

## Creating an Assignment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Assignment form">
    Navigate to your **Class**, select the **Assignments** tab, and click **New Assignment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a content type">
    Select one of four content types: **Lesson**, **Simulation**, **Quiz**, or **Custom**. Your choice determines which content appears in the search results in the next step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search and select content">
    Use the search bar or filters (topic, grade level, standard) to find the content you want to assign. Click the item to preview it, then click **Select** to add it to the assignment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the title, description, and due date">
    Enter an **assignment title** (defaults to the content name, but you can customize it), an optional **description** with any instructions you want students to read before starting, and a **due date and time**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who receives the assignment">
    Select **Whole class** to assign to everyone, or switch to **Specific students** and check off individual names. This is useful for enrichment work, make-up assignments, or differentiated tasks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a point value (optional)">
    Enter a **point value** if you want this assignment to count toward grades in EcoLearn's gradebook. Leave it blank if the assignment is ungraded practice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the assignment">
    Click **Publish** to send the assignment to students immediately, or use **Scheduled Publish** (see tip below) to release it at a future date and time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assignment Types

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Lesson Assignment" icon="book-open">
    Students read through a structured lesson module covering a specific ecology concept — for example, trophic levels or nutrient cycles — with embedded check-for-understanding questions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Simulation Assignment" icon="flask">
    Students run an interactive simulation with parameters you define, observe outcomes, and respond to guided reflection questions. Ideal for hypothesis-testing and data-analysis tasks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Quiz Assignment" icon="clipboard-check">
    A targeted assessment drawn from EcoLearn's question bank or your own custom questions. Supports multiple choice, matching, and short answer formats.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Custom Assignment" icon="pencil">
    Combine any EcoLearn lessons, simulations, or quizzes with your own questions and instructions into a single multi-part assignment tailored to your class.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Tracking Submissions

From the **Assignments** tab in your class, select any assignment to open the **Submission Tracker**. Each student's row displays their current status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Not Started** | The student has not opened the assignment yet. |
| **In Progress** | The student has opened and started but not submitted. |
| **Submitted** | The student has submitted and is awaiting grading. |
| **Graded** | A score has been recorded (auto or manual). |

Click any student's name to open their individual submission and review exactly what they submitted, including simulation run data and written responses.

## Late Work Policy

Configure your late work policy per assignment under **Assignment Settings → Late Work**:

- **Grace period** — set a window after the due date (in hours or days) during which late submissions are still accepted without penalty.
- **Accept / reject late submissions** — toggle this to stop accepting submissions entirely after the grace period ends, or leave it on to accept late work indefinitely.
- **Automatic point deduction** — set a percentage deduction applied to late submissions (for example, 10% per day late). EcoLearn applies this automatically when you grade.

<Tip>
  Use the **Scheduled Publish** option to prepare a whole week's worth of assignments in advance and have them release automatically on the days you choose. Find it in the Publish dropdown when creating or editing an assignment.
</Tip>
