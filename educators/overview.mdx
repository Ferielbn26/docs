EcoLearn is built with educators in mind. Whether you're teaching a middle school unit on food webs or guiding AP Environmental Science students through ecosystem modeling, EcoLearn's classroom tools cut down your prep time, keep students engaged with interactive simulations, and ensure every lesson maps to the standards your curriculum requires. From your Educator Dashboard you can create classes, build and publish assignments, review student work, and pull grades directly into your LMS — all in one place.

## Educator Account Verification

EcoLearn requires all educator accounts to be verified before you can access classroom tools. Verification confirms your professional status and keeps the platform safe for students. You can complete verification in one of two ways:

- **School email domain** — if you sign up with an email address on a recognized K-12 or higher-education domain (for example, `@district.k12.us` or `@university.edu`), your account is automatically flagged for expedited review.
- **Document upload** — if you use a personal email address, upload a scanned copy of a current staff ID, employment letter, or class schedule during signup. The EcoLearn Trust & Safety team reviews submissions within one business day.

To request verification, select **Educator Account** during the initial signup flow and follow the on-screen prompts. If you've already created a standard account, go to **Account Settings → Educator Verification** to submit your credentials.

<Note>
  Educators on the **Pro plan** can create up to 10 classes and enroll up to 300 students in total. Upgrade to the **School plan** to remove both limits and unlock school-wide sharing, admin dashboards, and priority support.
</Note>
