EcoLearn is an interactive, web-based ecology education platform designed for students, self-learners, and educators who want to explore the natural world through hands-on digital experiences. Whether you are a high school student diving into food webs for the first time, a college learner deepening your understanding of biodiversity, or a teacher building a standards-aligned curriculum, EcoLearn meets you where you are. With immersive simulations, guided lessons, and real-time progress tracking, EcoLearn transforms abstract ecological concepts into engaging, actionable learning that sticks.

## How EcoLearn Works

Getting started with EcoLearn takes only a few minutes. Follow these four steps to go from sign-up to your first learning experience.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Visit [app.ecolearn.app](https://app.ecolearn.app) and sign up using your email address or through Google or Microsoft single sign-on. Your account is free to create and ready to use immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Path">
    Select the role that best fits how you will use EcoLearn — **Student** or **Educator**. Your choice shapes your dashboard experience, the tools available to you, and how your progress is organized.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore Lessons">
    Browse the lesson library and pick a topic that interests you, such as Terrestrial Ecosystems, Marine Biodiversity, or Climate Science. Each lesson combines reading, interactive activities, and knowledge checks to reinforce what you learn.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track Your Progress">
    View your Progress dashboard at any time to see completed lessons, simulation runs, quiz scores, and learning streaks. Educators can also monitor class-wide performance from their dedicated instructor view.
  </Step>
</Steps>
