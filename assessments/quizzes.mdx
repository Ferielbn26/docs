Every lesson you complete in EcoLearn ends with a short quiz designed to reinforce what you just learned and check your understanding before you move on. Quizzes are a core part of the learning flow — not just a checkpoint, but an opportunity to solidify new concepts, identify any gaps, and earn rewards for your effort. If you don't pass on your first attempt, don't worry: you can retake any quiz as many times as you need to, and only your highest score is ever recorded.

## Quiz Types

EcoLearn offers four types of quizzes to support different stages of your learning journey.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Lesson Quiz" icon="circle-check">
    A 5–10 question quiz at the end of every lesson. Covers the core concepts from that lesson and must be passed to unlock the next one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Module Assessment" icon="clipboard-list">
    A 20-question test at the end of each module. Tests your knowledge across all lessons in that module and counts toward your overall module completion.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Adaptive Quiz" icon="sliders">
    Automatically adjusts its difficulty based on how you're performing mid-quiz. Questions get harder when you're on a roll and ease up when you need more support.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Practice Quiz" icon="book-open">
    Untimed and ungraded, available anytime for self-study. Great for reviewing a topic before a Module Assessment or just exploring what you know.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Question Formats

Quizzes use a variety of question formats to keep things engaging and test different types of ecological understanding:

- **Multiple choice** — select the best answer from four options.
- **True/false** — decide whether a statement about ecology is correct.
- **Drag-and-drop matching** — pair terms, organisms, or processes with their correct descriptions or categories.
- **Image-based identification** — look at a photo or illustration and answer questions about it (for example, "Identify this ecosystem" or "Name this species").
- **Short answer** — type a brief written response; these are marked by EcoLearn's built-in AI and reviewed for key concepts.

## Scoring and Feedback

Your score for each quiz is calculated as a simple percentage of questions answered correctly. As soon as you submit, you'll see your score alongside detailed feedback for every question you got wrong — including an explanation of the correct answer and why it matters. This instant feedback is one of the most valuable parts of the quiz experience, so take a moment to read through it even if you passed.

To unlock the next lesson or module, you need to achieve a **passing score of 70% or higher**. If you score below 70%, the next content will remain locked until you retake the quiz and meet the threshold.

<Note>
  Quiz results — including your scores and feedback history — are visible to your teacher if you are enrolled in a class. Practice Quizzes are never shared with teachers.
</Note>

## Retaking Quizzes

You can retake any graded quiz as many times as you like. To start a retake:

1. Navigate to your **Results** page from the left sidebar.
2. Find the quiz you want to redo and click **Retake Quiz**.

Each retake pulls from a different set of questions drawn from the same topic pool, so you're always practicing with fresh material rather than memorising the same answers. Only your **highest score** across all attempts is saved to your profile and shown to your teacher — earlier attempts are not counted against you.

## Badges and XP

EcoLearn rewards your quiz efforts with experience points (XP) and collectible badges. XP accumulates across your entire account and contributes to your learner level, while badges are displayed on your public profile as milestones of achievement.

| Achievement | XP Reward |
|---|---|
| Lesson Quiz Pass | 50 XP |
| First-Attempt Perfect Score | 100 XP |
| Module Assessment Pass | 200 XP |
| 7-Day Streak | 150 XP |

Bonus XP is awarded the first time you pass a quiz with a perfect score — a great incentive to really nail it on your first try. Streak badges are earned by completing at least one lesson or quiz every day; keeping your streak alive is one of the fastest ways to build up XP.
