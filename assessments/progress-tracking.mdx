EcoLearn's progress dashboard gives you a complete, at-a-glance picture of your learning journey. From the moment you complete your first lesson, every quiz score, earned badge, time-on-task, and skill unlocked is recorded and displayed in one central place. Whether you want to celebrate how far you've come, spot a topic that needs more attention, or prepare a report for a teacher or advisor, the progress dashboard is your go-to hub for understanding exactly where you stand.

## Your Progress Dashboard

Navigating to your dashboard takes just a couple of clicks.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your account menu">
    Click your **avatar** (your profile picture or initials) in the top-right corner of any EcoLearn page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to My Progress">
    Select **My Progress** from the dropdown menu. Your personal dashboard loads immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once you're on the dashboard, you'll find four key sections:

- **Overall completion %** — a progress ring showing how much of your enrolled course or curriculum you've finished.
- **Topics covered** — a count and list of every ecology topic you've engaged with so far.
- **Recent activity** — a chronological feed of your last completed lessons, quiz attempts, and earned badges.
- **Badges earned** — a visual gallery of every achievement badge on your account, with locked badges shown in greyscale so you know what to aim for next.

## Skill Mastery Map

Below your summary statistics you'll find the **Skill Mastery Map** — an interactive visual web that lays out all the ecology topics in EcoLearn and shows your relationship with each one. Each topic appears as a node, and the connections between nodes reflect real-world ecological relationships (for example, "Food Webs" connects to "Producers & Consumers" and "Energy Flow").

Topic nodes are colour-coded to reflect your current mastery level:

- 🟢 **Green** — mastered. You've completed all lessons and passed all assessments for this topic.
- 🟡 **Yellow** — in progress. You've started this topic but haven't finished all its lessons or passed its module assessment yet.
- ⚪ **Grey** — not started. This topic is available but you haven't engaged with it yet.

Click any node on the map to jump directly to that topic's lesson list, or hover over it to see a quick summary of your score history for that area.

## Learning Streak

Your **learning streak** counts the number of consecutive days you've engaged with EcoLearn. To keep your streak alive, complete at least one lesson or quiz each day — it doesn't matter how short the activity is, as long as you do something. Your current streak is displayed prominently at the top of your dashboard and in the app's navigation bar as a daily reminder.

Maintaining your streak unlocks special rewards at key milestones:

| Streak Milestone | Reward |
|---|---|
| 7 days | 150 XP + **Week Warrior** badge |
| 30 days | 500 XP + **Monthly Naturalist** badge |
| 100 days | 1,500 XP + **Century Scholar** badge |

<Note>
  If you miss a day, your streak resets to zero. There are no streak freezes in EcoLearn, so try to build the daily habit — even five minutes counts.
</Note>

## Downloading Your Progress Report

You can export a detailed PDF summary of your learning history at any time — useful for school portfolios, parent check-ins, or your own records.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Reports tab">
    From your **My Progress** dashboard, click the **Reports** tab near the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a date range">
    Use the date picker to choose the start and end dates you want the report to cover. You can select a custom range or use a preset like "Last 30 days" or "This semester."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the PDF">
    Click **Download PDF**. Your report will generate and download to your device within a few seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your downloaded report includes:

- **Lesson completion** — every lesson you finished within the selected date range.
- **Quiz scores** — your highest score for each quiz, plus the date it was achieved.
- **Time spent** — total hours and minutes logged on the platform.
- **Skills mastered** — a list of all ecology topics you achieved green (mastered) status on.

<Tip>
  Share your PDF progress report with a parent, teacher, or academic advisor to keep them informed of your achievements and get support for any areas where you're still working toward mastery.
</Tip>

## Class Progress (Student View)

If your teacher has added you to a class in EcoLearn, you gain access to an extra tab on your dashboard that lets you see how you're doing relative to your peers.

To view class-level data, go to **My Progress → Class View tab**. Here you'll see anonymized class averages for quiz scores, lesson completion rates, and overall progress — your classmates' names and individual scores are never revealed. Use these averages as a motivating benchmark: if the class average on a module assessment is 74% and you scored 88%, you know your hard work is paying off. If you're below the average, it's a helpful nudge to revisit the lessons or try a Practice Quiz before your next attempt.
