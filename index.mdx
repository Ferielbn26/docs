EcoLearn is an interactive web platform for ecology education designed for students from grade 5 through university and the educators who guide them. Whether you're a student curious about the natural world or a teacher building a curriculum, EcoLearn gives you the tools to explore ecosystems, run real-time simulations, track your learning progress, and manage classes — all in one place.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Sign up at [app.ecolearn.app](https://app.ecolearn.app) and choose your role — Student or Educator — to get a personalized experience from day one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore Lessons & Simulations">
    Browse 200+ lessons spanning terrestrial, aquatic, and urban ecosystems, each paired with interactive simulations and visual explainers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test Your Knowledge">
    Take a quiz after each lesson to reinforce what you've learned. Earn XP and unlock badges for great scores.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track Your Growth">
    Your progress dashboard shows skill mastery, learning streaks, and achievements so you always know where you stand.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Educators:** Set up your first class, assign lessons, and export grades to Google Classroom or Canvas. Visit [For Educators](/educators/overview) to get started.
</Note>
