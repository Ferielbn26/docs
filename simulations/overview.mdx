EcoLearn's simulations give you the ability to do something no textbook can: change the rules of an ecosystem and watch what happens. Rather than reading about how predator and prey populations cycle through boom-and-bust phases, you can set the parameters yourself and observe the outcomes unfold in real time. Rather than studying deforestation statistics on a page, you can convert a virtual forest and watch biodiversity collapse — then try restoration and see what recovers. Simulations are tightly linked to the lesson library, so you'll encounter them at natural points in your modules as a way to turn conceptual understanding into direct, hands-on experience.

## How to Use a Simulation

Every simulation in EcoLearn follows the same general workflow. Once you're familiar with the steps below, you'll be able to jump into any simulation with confidence.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Simulations Tab">
    From the EcoLearn home screen, click **Simulations** in the top navigation bar to open the simulations library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Simulation">
    Browse the available simulations and click the one you want to run. Each card shows a brief description and the estimated time to complete a full run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the Scenario Description">
    Before adjusting any controls, read the scenario description carefully. It explains the ecological context, the question you're investigating, and any background information you need to interpret your results meaningfully.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust Parameters Using Sliders and Controls">
    Use the on-screen sliders, dropdowns, and input fields to set your starting variables. Each control has a tooltip — hover over the label to see a plain-language explanation of what that parameter does.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Run">
    When your parameters are set, click the **Run** button to start the simulation. Some simulations run in a few seconds; others animate over a longer period to show change over decades or centuries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Results Graph or Map">
    Once the simulation completes, examine the output — this may be a time-series graph, a biodiversity index chart, or an animated habitat map. Use the zoom and annotation tools to explore specific points in the data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the Reflection Questions">
    Each simulation ends with a set of reflection questions that ask you to interpret your results, consider what would happen if you changed a variable, and connect your findings back to real-world ecological issues. Submit your responses to complete the simulation activity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Saving and Sharing Results

EcoLearn makes it easy to save your simulation runs and share them with classmates or your teacher.

**To save a run:** After a simulation completes, click the **Save** button in the results panel. You'll be prompted to give your run a name — choose something descriptive, like "Wolf-Deer High Predation Rate" — so you can easily identify it later. All saved runs appear under **My Simulations** in your learner profile.

**To share with your teacher or class:** Open a saved run from **My Simulations**, then click **Share**. You can either copy a direct link to send manually or use **Send to Class** to push the run directly to your enrolled class, where your teacher and classmates can view and comment on it.

**To export as PDF:** From any completed simulation results page, click **Export → Download as PDF**. The PDF includes your parameter settings, result graphs, and your submitted reflection responses — making it easy to include simulation work in a lab report or assignment submission.

<Info>
  Simulations are designed to run smoothly on **desktop and laptop computers** for the fullest experience, including full-resolution graphs and side-by-side comparison views. That said, all simulations are also mobile-friendly — core controls, graphs, and reflection questions are fully accessible on phones and tablets, so you can run a simulation from anywhere.
</Info>
