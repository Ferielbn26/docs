Your EcoLearn account is the foundation of your entire learning experience. EcoLearn offers two distinct roles — **Student** and **Educator** — each with its own dashboard, tools, and feature set tailored to how you use the platform. This guide walks you through creating and configuring your account, setting up your profile, connecting single sign-on providers, and securing your login. Choose the tab below that matches your role to get started.

## Create Your Account

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Student Account">
    Follow these steps to create and configure a Student account on EcoLearn.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Sign Up">
        Go to [app.ecolearn.app](https://app.ecolearn.app) and click **Sign Up**. Enter your email address and create a password, or continue with Google or Microsoft SSO. Confirm your email address by clicking the link sent to your inbox — your account activates immediately.

        On the role-selection screen, choose **Student** to set up your account with the student-focused dashboard and feature set.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set Your Grade Level">
        After your first login, EcoLearn prompts you to select your current grade level. This helps the platform recommend age-appropriate lessons, surface relevant simulations, and match the correct curriculum standards for your region.

        You can update your grade level at any time from **Account → Profile Settings**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Join a Class with a Class Code">
        If your teacher uses EcoLearn, they will provide you with a **Class Code** — a short alphanumeric string unique to your class. To join:

        1. Open your Dashboard and click **Join a Class**.
        2. Enter the Class Code your teacher shared and click **Join**.
        3. Your account is now linked to your class roster. Assigned lessons and due dates will appear automatically in your **Assignments** tab.

        You can be a member of multiple classes at once if you have codes for each.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set Your Learning Goals">
        Navigate to **Account → Profile Settings → Learning Goals**. Choose one or more preset goals — such as *AP Environmental Science Prep*, *Explore Marine Ecosystems*, or *Build Climate Literacy* — or type a custom goal. EcoLearn uses your goals to personalize lesson recommendations and progress milestones.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Educator Account">
    Follow these steps to create and configure an Educator account on EcoLearn.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Sign Up">
        Go to [app.ecolearn.app](https://app.ecolearn.app) and click **Sign Up**. Register with your email address or via Google or Microsoft SSO. On the role-selection screen, choose **Educator** to unlock class management, assignment tools, and student analytics.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify Your Educator Status">
        To confirm your educator status, EcoLearn offers two verification methods:

        - **School Email Address** — if you registered with an email ending in a recognized institutional domain (e.g., `.edu` or a district domain), verification is automatic.
        - **Upload Verification Document** — if you used a personal email, go to **Account → Educator Verification** and upload a photo of your school-issued ID, a pay stub, or an official staff letter. The EcoLearn team reviews submissions within one business day and notifies you by email when approved.

        Until verification is complete, your account functions as a standard Student account.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set Up Your First Class">
        Once verified, navigate to **Dashboard → My Classes → Create Class**. Fill in the following details:

        - **Class Name** — for example, *Period 3 – Environmental Science*.
        - **Grade Level and Subject** — used to align lesson recommendations to your curriculum.
        - **Class Code** — EcoLearn auto-generates a unique code you can share with students. You can regenerate it at any time.

        After creating your class, you can assign lessons, schedule simulations, and begin monitoring student progress from the **Instructor View**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Profile Settings

Keeping your profile up to date ensures EcoLearn personalizes your experience correctly and that classmates and educators recognize you. To access your profile, click your avatar in the top-right corner and select **Profile Settings**.

- **Display Name** — update the name shown on your dashboard, progress certificates, and class rosters.
- **Profile Picture** — upload a photo or choose from the EcoLearn avatar library. Supported formats are JPG, PNG, and GIF (max 5 MB).
- **Notification Preferences** — choose which email or in-app notifications you receive, including new assignments, lesson reminders, simulation updates, and platform announcements.
- **Language** — select your preferred interface language from the dropdown. EcoLearn currently supports English, Spanish, French, and Portuguese.

## SSO Sign-In

EcoLearn supports single sign-on (SSO) through the following providers, so you can log in without managing a separate password.

- **Google** — sign in with any personal or Google Workspace (school) account.
- **Microsoft** — sign in with a Microsoft personal account or a Microsoft 365 school or work account.

**To link an SSO provider to your existing EcoLearn account:**

1. Go to **Account → Security → Linked Accounts**.
2. Click **Connect** next to the provider you want to link.
3. Follow the OAuth prompt to authorize EcoLearn. Once linked, you can sign in using that provider from the login screen.

You can link both providers simultaneously and use either one to access your account. To unlink a provider, return to **Linked Accounts** and click **Disconnect** — make sure you have either another linked provider or a password set before disconnecting to avoid being locked out.

## Account Security

Protecting your EcoLearn account keeps your progress, assignments, and personal information safe.

**Changing Your Password**

1. Navigate to **Account → Security → Change Password**.
2. Enter your current password, then type and confirm your new password.
3. Click **Save Changes**. You will receive a confirmation email, and all other active sessions will be signed out automatically.

**Enabling Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**

Two-factor authentication adds a second layer of security by requiring a one-time code in addition to your password at sign-in.

1. Go to **Account → Security → Two-Factor Authentication** and click **Enable 2FA**.
2. Choose your preferred method: **Authenticator App** (recommended — works with Google Authenticator, Authy, and similar apps) or **SMS**.
3. Follow the on-screen setup steps and save your backup codes in a secure location.

Once enabled, you will be prompted for your 2FA code each time you log in from a new device or browser.

<Warning>
  Deleting your EcoLearn account is permanent and cannot be undone. All of your lesson progress, simulation history, quiz scores, and assignments will be erased immediately and cannot be recovered. If you are a student, your educator will also lose access to your historical performance data. If you simply need a break, consider logging out rather than deleting your account. To request account deletion, go to **Account → Settings → Delete Account** and follow the confirmation steps.
</Warning>
