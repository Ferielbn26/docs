Terrestrial ecosystems cover most of Earth's land surface and support an extraordinary diversity of life — from the towering canopies of tropical rainforests to the sparse, wind-swept expanses of Arctic tundra. EcoLearn's terrestrial lessons take you inside each of these biomes to understand how climate, soil, plants, and animals interact to create stable, dynamic systems. Along the way, you'll also explore how human activities are reshaping these environments and what that means for species and communities that depend on them.

## What You'll Learn

By working through the Terrestrial Ecosystems modules, you will build knowledge and skills across the following areas:

- **Biome types and distribution** — how temperature, precipitation, and latitude determine which biome exists where
- **Soil ecology** — the role of soil composition, decomposers, and nutrient cycling in sustaining plant and animal life
- **Plant adaptations** — how vegetation evolves specialized structures and strategies to thrive in specific conditions
- **Animal behavior and adaptation** — how terrestrial animals forage, migrate, hibernate, and compete within their ecosystems
- **Deforestation and land-use impacts** — how habitat loss, fragmentation, and degradation affect biodiversity and ecosystem services

## Module Breakdown

Each module in the Terrestrial Ecosystems category focuses on one major biome type. The table below gives you an at-a-glance overview of what to expect in each.

| Module      | Key Topics                              | Activities                          | Duration  |
|-------------|-----------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|-----------|
| Forests     | Carbon storage, deforestation           | Canopy explorer, tree ID challenge  | ~45 min   |
| Grasslands  | Savanna vs. prairie ecosystems          | Grass species quiz                  | ~30 min   |
| Deserts     | Water conservation, extreme adaptations | Cactus anatomy activity             | ~30 min   |
| Tundra      | Permafrost, Arctic species              | Permafrost distribution map         | ~25 min   |

Modules are designed to be completed independently, so you can start with whichever biome interests you most. However, completing them in order provides a natural progression from the most biodiverse to the most extreme environments.

## How to Start

Follow these steps to begin your first Terrestrial Ecosystems lesson.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Lessons">
    From the EcoLearn home screen, click **Lessons** in the top navigation bar to open the lesson library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Terrestrial Ecosystems">
    On the Lessons Overview page, click the **Terrestrial Ecosystems** card to enter the module category.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Module">
    Browse the available modules — Forests, Grasslands, Deserts, and Tundra — and click the one you want to explore first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Begin">
    On the module overview page, click **Begin Module** to start the first lesson. EcoLearn will guide you through each lesson, activity, and quiz in sequence.
  </Step>
</Steps>
