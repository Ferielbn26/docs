EcoLearn is built with privacy as a foundation, not an afterthought. We are committed to being transparent about what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe — especially when it comes to students and young learners. This page explains our data practices in plain language and tells you exactly how to exercise your privacy rights.

## Data We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate the platform, personalize your learning experience, and help educators support their students. The table below summarizes what we collect and why.

| Data Type                                        | Why We Collect It                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Account info (name, email)                       | Authentication and communication                           |
| Learning progress (lessons, quiz scores)         | Personalized learning and teacher reports                  |
| Usage analytics (time on platform, clicks)       | Improving the product — anonymized and never sold          |
| Device / browser info                            | Technical support and compatibility                        |

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as Social Security numbers, financial data (payment is handled by our third-party processor), or precise geolocation.

## Compliance

EcoLearn is designed to meet the requirements of major education privacy laws. We actively review and update our practices as regulations evolve.

**COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)**
EcoLearn does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. Student accounts for users under 13 must be created by a parent or by an educator through a school account, where the school assumes responsibility for obtaining appropriate consent.

**FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act)**
Student education records created within EcoLearn are protected under FERPA. Schools that use EcoLearn under an institutional license are considered the data controller for their students' records. EcoLearn acts as a service provider and will not disclose student records without authorization from the school, except as required by law.

**GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)**
Users in the European Union have the right to access, correct, export, and delete their personal data at any time. EcoLearn's data processing infrastructure complies with GDPR requirements, including data minimization, purpose limitation, and lawful basis for processing. EU users can submit data requests directly through the platform (see **Your Data Rights** below).

## Student Data Protection

EcoLearn takes additional precautions specifically for student accounts:

- **We do not sell student data** — ever. Student information is never used for advertising or shared with third parties for commercial purposes.
- **Student accounts for users under 13** can only be created by a parent or guardian, or by an educator through an institutional account where the school has accepted our Data Processing Agreement.
- **Teachers and school administrators** can request a **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** by contacting **privacy@ecolearn.app**. The DPA outlines our obligations as a data processor and supports your institution's FERPA and GDPR compliance.
- **Educators can view** their students' progress, quiz scores, and activity within their assigned classes — but cannot access personal account details beyond name and email.

<Info>
  If you are a school administrator evaluating EcoLearn for district-wide use, our full Privacy Policy, DPA template, and security documentation are available on request. Email **privacy@ecolearn.app** with the subject line "Security Review Request."
</Info>

## Your Data Rights

You have meaningful control over your personal data. Use the options below to export, delete, or request a copy of your information.

<Accordion title="Export My Data">
  You can download a complete export of your EcoLearn data at any time:

  1. Go to **Account Settings → Privacy → Export Data**.
  2. Click **Request Export**. EcoLearn will prepare your data archive.
  3. You will receive an email with a download link within 24 hours.
  4. The export is provided as a **JSON file** containing your profile information, learning progress, quiz scores, and activity history.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Delete My Data">
  Deleting your account permanently removes all associated personal data from EcoLearn's systems:

  1. Go to **Account Settings → Privacy → Delete Account**.
  2. Read the confirmation notice carefully — deletion cannot be undone.
  3. Enter your password and click **Permanently Delete My Account**.

  All personal data is purged from active systems within **30 days** of deletion. See the **Data Retention** section below for full details.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Request a Copy or Correction">
  If you need a formal copy of your data for legal or compliance purposes, or if you believe your data contains an inaccuracy, contact us directly:

  - Email: **privacy@ecolearn.app**
  - Subject line: "Data Access Request" or "Data Correction Request"
  - Include your registered account email and a description of the information you're requesting or correcting.

  We respond to all privacy requests within **5 business days**.
</Accordion>

## Data Retention

| Account Status    | Retention Policy                                                                 |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Active account    | All data is retained for as long as your account remains open.                   |
| Deleted account   | All personal data is purged from active systems within **30 days** of deletion.  |
| Inactive account  | Accounts with no activity for 3 years may be archived; you will be notified by email before any action is taken. |

Anonymized, aggregated analytics data (e.g., overall lesson completion rates) may be retained indefinitely as it cannot be linked back to any individual.
