Your EcoLearn account settings give you full control over your profile, security credentials, connected services, and how the platform communicates with you. Whether you're a student personalizing your learning experience or an educator keeping your contact details up to date, everything you need is a few clicks away.

## Accessing Account Settings

To open your account settings, click your **avatar** in the top-right corner of any EcoLearn page and select **Account Settings** from the dropdown menu. All the sections described below are found within this settings panel.

## Updating Your Profile

Keep your profile accurate so classmates, educators, and the platform can identify you correctly.

1. Navigate to **Account Settings → Profile**.
2. Update your **display name** — this is the name visible to teachers and classmates.
3. Add or edit your **bio** to introduce yourself or describe your teaching focus.
4. Upload a **profile picture** by clicking the avatar placeholder and selecting a JPG or PNG file. The maximum file size is **5 MB**.
5. Set your **grade level** (students) or **subject area** (educators) so EcoLearn can surface relevant content recommendations.
6. Click **Save Changes** when you're done.

## Changing Email or Password

You can update your login credentials at any time from the Security section.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security Settings">
    Go to **Account Settings → Security**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Current Password">
    Type your existing password to verify your identity before making any changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your New Email or Password">
    Type your new email address or new password in the respective field. Passwords must be at least 8 characters and include at least one number or special character.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm via Verification Email">
    EcoLearn will send a confirmation link to your new (or existing) email address. Click the link to apply the change. The link expires after 24 hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you use Google or Microsoft SSO to log in, your password is managed by that provider. Visit your Google or Microsoft account settings to change it.
</Note>

## Connected Accounts

EcoLearn supports single sign-on (SSO) through **Google** and **Microsoft**, letting you log in without a separate password.

**To link an SSO provider:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Connected Accounts">
    Navigate to **Account Settings → Connected Accounts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Provider">
    Click **Link Google Account** or **Link Microsoft Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the Connection">
    A pop-up window will appear. Sign in to your Google or Microsoft account and grant EcoLearn the requested permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Link">
    Once authorized, the provider will appear as **Connected** under your account. You can now use it to sign in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**To unlink an SSO provider**, click **Disconnect** next to the provider name. Make sure your account has a password set before disconnecting so you don't lose access.

<Warning>
  If SSO is your only login method and you disconnect it without setting a password first, you will be locked out of your account. Set a password under **Account Settings → Security** before unlinking.
</Warning>

## Notification Preferences

Control which notifications EcoLearn sends you by visiting **Account Settings → Notifications**. The table below shows all available notification types and their default states.

| Notification Type        | Where to Configure                      | Default |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------- | ------- |
| Assignment due reminders | Account Settings → Notifications        | On      |
| Quiz results             | Account Settings → Notifications        | On      |
| Class announcements      | Account Settings → Notifications        | On      |
| Weekly progress digest   | Account Settings → Notifications        | Off     |
| Marketing emails         | Account Settings → Notifications        | Off     |

Toggle any notification on or off using the switches on the Notifications page, then click **Save Preferences**.

## Deleting Your Account

If you wish to permanently close your EcoLearn account, you can do so from **Account Settings → Account → Delete Account**.

<Warning>
  Account deletion is **permanent and cannot be undone**. All of your learning progress, earned badges, saved simulations, class memberships, and personal data will be immediately and irreversibly removed. If you are an educator, your classes and associated student data will also be deleted. Download any data you want to keep before proceeding (see **Privacy & Data** for export instructions).
</Warning>

You will be asked to type your password and confirm your decision before deletion proceeds.
