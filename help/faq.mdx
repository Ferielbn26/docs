## Getting Started

<Accordion title="Is EcoLearn free to use?">
  Yes! EcoLearn offers a **Free plan** that gives you access to 20 lessons and 2 interactive simulations at no cost — no credit card required. If you want to unlock the full library of 200+ lessons, all simulations, and advanced features, you can upgrade to a Student, Pro Educator, or School plan at any time. Visit the [Subscription Plans](/help/subscription-plans) page to compare options.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What age group is EcoLearn for?">
  EcoLearn is designed for learners aged **10 and up**. The platform includes courses for **K–12 students** as well as introductory and advanced content suited to **higher education**. Content is tagged by grade level so you always know what's appropriate for your course or classroom.

  Students under 13 require verifiable **parental consent** before creating an account, in compliance with COPPA. Parents can create an account on their child's behalf, or an educator can provision student accounts through a school license.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need to install anything?">
  No installation is required. EcoLearn runs entirely in your **web browser** — just visit the site and log in. We support all major modern browsers:

  - Google Chrome (version 90 or later)
  - Mozilla Firefox (version 88 or later)
  - Apple Safari (version 14 or later)
  - Microsoft Edge (latest version)

  A **mobile app** is also available for **iOS** and **Android** if you prefer learning on a phone or tablet. Search for "EcoLearn" in the App Store or Google Play.
</Accordion>

## Lessons & Simulations

<Accordion title="Can I redo a lesson I've already completed?">
  Absolutely. Revisiting completed lessons is a great way to reinforce what you've learned. From your **Progress Dashboard**, find the lesson you want to revisit and click on it. You'll see two options:

  - **Review** — plays through the lesson again with your saved notes and highlights intact.
  - **Restart** — clears your progress for that lesson and starts fresh, useful if you want to retake the embedded quiz.

  Restarting a lesson does not remove it from your overall completion history or affect your badges.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I save my simulation results?">
  After completing or running a simulation, click the **Save Run** button in the simulation toolbar. You'll be prompted to give the run a name so you can identify it later. All saved runs are accessible from **Simulations → My Saved Runs** in the main navigation.

  <Tip>
    Name your runs descriptively (for example, "Forest Fire — High Wind Scenario") so they're easy to find when writing up your results or submitting to a teacher.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Are new lessons added regularly?">
  Yes! The EcoLearn content team adds **new lessons and simulations every month**, covering emerging ecology topics, seasonal content, and curriculum updates. To see what's been added recently, check the **What's New** page in your dashboard — it's updated with each content release and includes a short description of new material.
</Accordion>

## Educators

<Accordion title="How do students join my class?">
  Every class you create in EcoLearn has a unique **6-character Class Join Code**. Share this code with your students verbally, in your LMS, or via email. Students join your class by:

  1. Logging in to EcoLearn (or creating a free account).
  2. Going to **Account Setup → Join a Class**.
  3. Entering the 6-character code and clicking **Join**.

  They'll appear in your class roster within seconds. You can view and regenerate your Join Code from **My Classes → [Class Name] → Settings**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I import grades into Google Classroom or Canvas?">
  Yes. EcoLearn supports grade export to major learning management systems. To export:

  1. Go to **Gradebook** in your educator dashboard.
  2. Click **Export**.
  3. Select your LMS format (Google Classroom, Canvas, Schoology, or CSV).
  4. Download the file and import it directly into your LMS.

  Full LMS integration — including automatic grade sync — is available on the **Pro Educator** and **School** plans. See [Subscription Plans](/help/subscription-plans) for details.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do students need paid plans if I have a Pro account?">
  No. When you assign content to students in your class, they can access **all assigned lessons and simulations regardless of their own plan tier**. Your Pro Educator or School plan covers your students' content access within your classes. Students on the Free plan outside of your class will still be limited to free-tier content.
</Accordion>

## Billing & Accounts

<Accordion title="What payment methods are accepted?">
  EcoLearn accepts the following payment methods for individual subscriptions:

  - Visa
  - Mastercard
  - American Express
  - PayPal

  For **School plan** customers, we also accept **purchase orders (POs)** issued by your institution. Contact **sales@ecolearn.app** to set up invoiced billing for your school or district.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I switch from monthly to annual billing?">
  Yes. You can switch to annual billing at any time to save **20% compared to the monthly rate**. To switch:

  1. Go to **Account Settings → Subscription**.
  2. Click **Switch to Annual Billing**.
  3. Review the prorated credit — any remaining days on your current monthly billing period will be applied as a credit toward your annual plan.
  4. Confirm the switch.

  Your new annual billing cycle begins immediately, and your renewal date updates accordingly.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to my progress if I downgrade to Free?">
  Your learning progress and all earned badges are **fully preserved** when you downgrade to the Free plan — nothing is deleted. However, you will lose access to premium lessons and simulations that are not included in the Free plan. Those lessons will appear as locked in your dashboard until you upgrade again.

  If you were an educator on a Pro plan, your class rosters and student data are also retained, but you will not be able to create new classes or access educator features until you upgrade.
</Accordion>
