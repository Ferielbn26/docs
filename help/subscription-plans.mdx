EcoLearn offers four subscription tiers designed to meet the needs of casual learners, dedicated students, classroom educators, and entire school districts. Every account starts with free access, and you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time. Read on to compare plans, understand what's included, and learn how to manage your subscription.

<Note>
  Annual billing is available on all paid plans and saves you **20% compared to monthly billing**. You can switch to annual billing at any time from your subscription settings.
</Note>

## Upgrading Your Plan

Upgrading takes just a few minutes and your new features are available immediately after payment is confirmed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Subscription Settings">
    Go to **Account Settings → Subscription** and click **Upgrade Plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Plan">
    Review the available plans and select the one that fits your needs. You can toggle between monthly and annual billing before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Payment Details">
    Enter your credit card information or select PayPal. All payment details are encrypted and processed securely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Your Upgrade">
    Click **Confirm Upgrade**. You'll receive a confirmation email with your receipt, and your new plan will activate instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you're an educator upgrading to Pro Educator, you can invite students to your class immediately after upgrading — students do not need a paid plan to access content you assign.
</Tip>

## School and Institution Licenses

The **School plan** is built for districts, schools, and higher-education institutions that need to deploy EcoLearn at scale. It includes everything in Pro Educator, plus:

- **Bulk pricing** based on the number of students enrolled — the more seats, the lower the per-student cost.
- **Single sign-on (SSO) setup** so staff and students can log in using your institution's existing credentials (Google Workspace, Microsoft Entra ID, and SAML 2.0 supported).
- **Admin dashboard** for managing all teacher and student accounts, viewing school-wide analytics, and controlling content visibility.
- **Dedicated onboarding support** and a named account manager.
- **Data processing agreements (DPAs)** to support FERPA and GDPR compliance requirements.

To request a quote or schedule a demo, contact the EcoLearn sales team at **sales@ecolearn.app** or click **Contact Sales** on the Pricing page.

## Cancelling Your Subscription

You can cancel your paid subscription at any time — no cancellation fees apply.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Subscription Settings">
    Go to **Account Settings → Subscription** and click **Cancel Subscription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Cancellation">
    Review the confirmation prompt, which shows the date your access will end, and click **Confirm Cancellation**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After cancellation, your paid access continues until the **end of the current billing period**. You will not be charged again after that date. When the period ends, your account automatically moves to the Free plan — your progress, badges, and saved data are fully retained.

<Note>
  Annual subscribers who cancel mid-year are not eligible for a prorated refund by default. Contact **support@ecolearn.app** within 14 days of your renewal date if you believe a refund is warranted.
</Note>
